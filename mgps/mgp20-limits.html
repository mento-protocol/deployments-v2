Mento governance · MGP-20

Proposed trading limit changes

Global-only (LG) trading limits on the ten USDm/FX exchanges left in Mento v2, re-sized from live supply. Each FX limit is 110% of the token's supply. Each USDm limit is the USD value of that amount at the pool's oracle rate. No limit goes below 10,000 USD: smaller pools get 10,000 USDm and 10,000 USD worth of the FX token. Every leg's netflow is reset to zero before the new limit is set.

Network Celo mainnet (42220) Sizing block 79,393,729 Units whole tokens Source MGP20.sol simulation output

What stands out

  • AUDm is exhausted on both legs. FX netflow is −1,595 of a 1,597 cap and USDm netflow is 1,144 of 1,144, so USDm → AUDm swaps revert today. Both limits rise about 9×.
  • XOFm already has more netflow than its new cap. FX netflow is 13,788,663 against a proposed cap of 5,903,258. Setting the smaller limit without the reset would block the pool, which is why every leg is reset first.
  • AUDm, CADm and ZARm are set by the 10,000 USD minimum. At 110% of supply they would be worth about 6,600, 750 and 740 USD. XOFm is just above the minimum at 10,125 USD.
  • The largest cuts are on ZARm (about −82%) and XOFm (about −72%), on both legs. COPm's supply (80.9M) is now above its current FX cap (75.1M), so its limits go up.
